# BizWise AI 💼

### Your AI-Powered Business Assistant

BizWise AI is an AI-powered assistant designed to help entrepreneurs plan, manage, and grow their businesses through practical guidance and data-driven insights.

## Features

- **Business Idea Generation:** Explore business ideas based on budget and customer needs.
- **Supplier Guidance:** Identify factors to compare when choosing suppliers.
- **Product Pricing:** Understand costs, pricing, and potential profit margins.
- **Location Suggestions:** Explore business location options based on customer needs and business type.
- **Inventory Management:** Track stock information and understand when to reorder.
- **Sales and Profit Analysis:** Calculate sales, expenses, and profit from provided figures.
- **Customer Feedback Analysis:** Identify common concerns and suggestions from customer reviews.
- **Conversational Assistance:** Ask business questions and receive guidance in one chat interface.

## How It Works

1. The user describes their business idea or challenge.
2. BizWise AI analyses the information provided.
3. It generates relevant suggestions, calculations, or recommendations.
4. The user can ask follow-up questions to explore the next steps.

## Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Install the dependencies listed in `requirements.txt`.
3. Configure the required environment variables using `.env.example`.
4. Follow the instructions for the actual agent entry-point file.
5. Run and test the agent in its intended environment.

## Limitations

BizWise AI provides decision-support guidance. Supplier availability, market prices, location suitability, and financial projections should be independently verified.

Automated inventory updates, sales records, or persistent financial tracking require the relevant data storage or integrations to be implemented.
